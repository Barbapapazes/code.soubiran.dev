# code.soubiran.dev

Create and share beautiful, syntax-highlighted images of your code.

[Open the editor](https://code.soubiran.dev), paste your code, customize the card, and select **Capture** to download a PNG. Share the URL to share the card.

You can also drag and drop an image onto the card (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, AVIF, or SVG, up to 5 MB), or drop another image to replace it. Customize the background and select **Capture** to download it as a PNG. Hover over the image or focus its remove button with the keyboard to reveal the rounded **×** at the top right; removing the image restores your code without losing its settings. Images stay in the current tab and are not included in shared URLs or sent to the assistant.

## MCP

Connect your agent to `https://code.soubiran.dev/mcp`. The public `generate_code_image` tool accepts `code` and optional `language`, `size`, `gradient`, `title`, and `watermark` values.
